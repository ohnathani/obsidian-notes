### Computer Network 
1. Personal Area Network (PAN) or Wireless PAN (WPAN)
	- the smallest computer-based networks
	- when your computer is connected to a device wired or wireless
	- e.g, printer, mice etc.
2. Body Area Network (BAN)
	- short range wireless network in, on, around body
	- e.g., smartwatch
3. Local Area Network (LAN)
	- group of computers and network devics connected together
		- max 100m
4. Campus Area Network (CAN)
	- multiple LAN connections
5. Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)
	- larger network, spans multiple buildings in same city or town
	- geographically dispersed LAN
	- uses optics fiber
6. Wide Area Network (WAN)
	- geographically distributed private telecom network
	- is an enterprise
	- LANs connect to WANs
7. The Internet
	- Connects computer network that connects computer networks and organizational computer facilities
