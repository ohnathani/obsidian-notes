# PART 2 — Cognition & Self

## Stephen Pinker

**Psychologist**

### How the Mind Works

- 1997
    

### Cognitive Rules

- Developmental arise
    
    - Accumulates through time
        
    - Kickstarts from parents (genetics)
        
- Ability of child >1 year old to realize humor
    
    - Scientific test on children
        
    - Must have something genetic
        
    - Count 1–3
        
- Inheritance and interaction plays a crucial role
    
- People become paternal to dumb people
    
    - Religion interferes with how you think that
        
- IQ and Love of God are separate but they can coexist
    
- Has many competing criteria
    

### Fission-Fusion Society

- Independent but can interact for mate
    
    - Like orangutans
        

### Mind

- Mind works similar to a computer
    
- **Computational Theory of the Mind**
    
- A lot of people don't agree
    

---

## Robert Penrose

- Disagrees with Pinker
    
- **Non-computational theory of mind**
    
- More religious than scientific
    
- More belief-based
    

### Pinker vs. Penrose

- **Pinker**
    
    - Computational theory of the mind
        
- **Penrose**
    
    - Non-computational theory of mind
