# 3. Friends and Society

### FRIENDS AND SOCIETY

- Friends are important in **shaping society**.
    
- Friendship involves **affinity by choice**.
    

### Chance vs. Choice

- **Chance** is an **imposed necessity**.
    
- Friends appear to be on the side of **choice**, but is friendship really entirely a choice?
    
    - You may meet someone by accident/chance.
        
    - Becoming friends is then based on choice.
        
    - What are the chances of you becoming friends with someone?
        
    - It depends on the **locale**.
        
        - Where you belong affects who you encounter.
            
- Therefore, friendship involves both **chance and choice**.
    

### Society of Friends

- Better to imagine a world with a **society of friends** than a society based only on family.

# 4. State and Society

### STATE AND SOCIETY

- **Republic of Plato**
    
    - Plato imagines a **communist society**.
        
    - Very particular/systematic.
        
    - Focuses on **nurturing the next generation**.
        
- After birth:
    
    - The child gets taken by the **government**.
        
    - The child thinks the **state is its parent**.
        
- The state checks **genetics**.
    
    - For what?
        
- The more you **plan**, the more you decrease **freedom**.
    

### Socialism and Planning

- **Socialist**
    
    - More planning is better.
        
- **Right side**
    
    - Government is followed with **less planning**.
        
- Government should only interfere in **drastic measures and needs**.
    
- Socialism isn't always good.
    

### Revolution

- **The Gulag Archipelago**
    
- Revolution isn't necessarily bad.
    
- Important questions:
    
    - What are the **intentions of the state**?
        
    - What are the **intentions of society**?
